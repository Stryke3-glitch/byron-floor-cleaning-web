02 · Conversion strategy

Primary CTA

"Get a Free Estimate" as the button label everywhere. The phone number , 908-691-5644 , is the secondary action and is shown as plain tappable text in the header, hero, and footer. On mobile, the header phone number is a tel: link with a phone icon.

Where forms appear

  • Homepage , short inline form just below the before/after section.
  • Services page , short inline form at the bottom.
  • /estimate , the form is the page.
  • Sticky bottom bar on mobile: "Call · Text · Estimate" , always one tap from action.

Trust elements (only what's true)

  • "25+ years of hands-on floor experience in NJ" , biggest trust lever you have. Use it in the hero. (Note: "with 25+ years of experience" reflects the owner's personal experience working with floors, not the legal age of the business , phrase it as "25+ years experience" rather than "in business with 25+ years of experience".)
  • Real before/after photos with location + floor type captions.
  • "24-hour reply to schedule your site visit" near the form. The actual quote happens after an in-person walkthrough , don't imply quotes go out within 24 hours.
  • "Free estimates · No obligation" repeated near every CTA.
  • Leave room for: Google reviews, insurance/bonding badge, client logos. Add as your uncle collects them. Don't fabricate any.

Mobile drop-off reduction

  • Sticky Call · Text · Estimate bar pinned to bottom of viewport (the dominant pattern that beats hero CTAs on phones).
  • Form fields: 8 max, large tap targets (min 48px), numeric keyboard for phone + sqft.
  • Phone number is always tappable (tel:) and SMS is offered (sms:).
  • Hero image lazy-loaded; LCP target < 2.5s on 4G.
  • No autoplaying anything. No popups.

05 · Visual direction

Three directions live in the prototype as a tweak. My pick is Modern Premium , keeps the gold/black DNA from the flyers but inverts it: bright, readable surfaces with gold as an accent, not a fill. Black/gold-everywhere reads as flashy in print but as cluttered and untrustworthy on a 5" screen.

Type

Headlines in Fraunces (a warm, slightly traditional serif , references the cursive of the flyers without being decorative). Body in Inter. Phone numbers and small UI in Inter as well. Two families only.

Color

  • Ink #0F1115 , primary text and dark surfaces
  • Paper #FAF8F3 , page background, warm off-white (not stark)
  • Gold #B8893A , accent only: button hover, underlines, dividers
  • Gold deep #8A6420 , links, hover states
  • Line #E7E4DC , borders, dividers

Buttons

Primary: solid black with white text, gold underline accent on hover. Secondary: outlined with the phone number prefix and a small phone glyph. No drop shadows. 4px radius , restrained, not pill-shaped.

Cards & sections

White cards on the warm-paper background, 1px line border, no shadow. Section dividers are a thin gold rule, 64px wide, centered. Imagery sits in 3:2 frames with a hairline border.

How to use black/gold tastefully

  • One dark section per page (the hero or the testimonials block, never both).
  • Gold only on type elements ≥ 18px or as solid backgrounds , small gold body text is unreadable.
  • No gradients, no metallic textures, no marble. The flyers do that , the website does the opposite.

07 · Before/after strategy

  • Drag-slider format on the homepage , single component, three pairs to start. Far more engaging than a side-by-side grid for the hero proof.
  • Caption format: "Office hallway · Edison, NJ · VCT, strip & wax · 1 night". Specific = credible.
  • No filtering at 3 pairs. Add filter chips (VCT / Tile / Vinyl / Office / School / Home) once you cross 8 pairs, and split into the dedicated /gallery page (the 4-page sitemap).
  • Mobile layout: stacked, full-width, swipeable. The slider handle should be at least 44px to be usable.
  • Proof copy under each: one short sentence , what was wrong, what we did, how long it took.

08 · Lead form

Form length: 8 fields, single page. Multi-step is not worth it for this category , the user is already motivated when they reach the form, and steps add abandonment without adding qualification value.

Anti-spam

  • Honeypot field (name="website") hidden via CSS , bots fill it, humans don't.
  • Time-trap: reject submissions that arrive < 2 seconds after page load.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile on the Apps Script endpoint if spam becomes a real problem (add later, not at launch).
  • Skip reCAPTCHA , it hurts conversion more than it helps for a low-volume local form.

Thank-you state

In-page replacement (no redirect , keeps you on the URL for analytics). Headline: "Got it. Talk soon." Body confirms 24-hour response window and offers the phone number for urgency. Include a "what happens next" 3-step micro-list to set expectations.

09 · Local SEO

Page titles

  • Home: Byron Floor Cleaning · Floor Stripping & Waxing · New Jersey
  • Services: Floor Stripping, Waxing & Deep Cleaning Services · Byron Floor Cleaning
  • Estimate: Get a Free Floor Cleaning Estimate · Byron Floor Cleaning · NJ

Meta descriptions

  • Home: "Floor stripping & waxing across New Jersey, backed by 25+ years of hands-on experience. Free in-person estimates. Call or text 908-691-5644."
  • Services: "VCT, tile, and vinyl floor care for offices, schools, stores, and homes throughout NJ. One-time and recurring service."
  • Estimate: "Request a free in-person floor cleaning estimate. We respond within 24 hours to schedule. Serving all of New Jersey."

Local keyword targets

  • "floor stripping and waxing new jersey" / "near me"
  • "vct floor cleaning [city]"
  • "commercial floor waxing nj"
  • "school floor cleaning nj"
  • "office floor stripping [county]"

Schema

Use LocalBusiness JSON-LD on every page with @type: "HomeAndConstructionBusiness" as a more specific child. Include name, telephone, areaServed (state of NJ), priceRange ("$$"), and a placeholder for hours. Add FAQPage schema on the homepage matching the visible FAQ.

Service-area content (without keyword spam)

One footer line , "Serving offices, schools, stores, and homes throughout New Jersey." Do not create one page per city. Instead, when you have a job in Union, write a short job-recap blog post (later) that earns a real local mention naturally. Google has gotten very good at penalizing doorway pages.

Google Business Profile (most important)

The website's local SEO ceiling is set by your Google Business Profile, not the site itself. Set it up with the real address (or service-area mode if your uncle works from home), real photos, weekly post updates, and ask every happy customer for a review with a direct review link. This will out-rank any on-page SEO work for local queries.

10 · Developer handoff (Claude Code)

Architecture

  • Static site. Three HTML files: index.html, services.html, estimate.html. Plus thanks.html (server-side fallback for users with JS off , JS path replaces in-place).
  • One shared styles.css, one main.js (≤ 8kb gzipped).
  • No build step required. Optional: Astro or 11ty if you want partials, but vanilla is fine for 3 pages.
  • Host: Cloudflare Pages or Netlify (free tier). Point GoDaddy DNS to it. SSL auto-provisioned.

Pages & components

  • SiteHeader , wordmark, phone, primary CTA
  • Hero , variant prop (modern / dark-luxury / clean-light)
  • BeforeAfterSlider , drag handle, keyboard accessible, lazy-loaded images
  • ServiceCard , five instances
  • ProcessSteps , three steps
  • EstimateForm , used on home and /estimate
  • FAQ , disclosure pattern, JSON-LD-friendly markup
  • SiteFooter
  • StickyMobileBar , Call · Text · Estimate, < 768px only

Responsive behavior

  • Mobile-first. Two breakpoints: 720px and 1080px.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44px. Form fields ≥ 48px. Body text ≥ 16px (no smaller than 14px anywhere).
  • Sticky bottom bar shown only ≤ 720px and only when the in-page form is not visible (IntersectionObserver).

Content model

Plain JSON in /content (services.json, faq.json, gallery.json) so future edits don't require touching HTML:

// services.json
[{
  "slug": "strip-wax-vct",
  "name": "Strip & Wax (VCT)",
  "summary": "Old wax stripped, multiple coats of commercial wax sealed in.",
  "details": "...",
  "icon": "wax"
}, ...]

Image handling

  • All photos served as <picture> with AVIF + WebP fallbacks, 3 widths (480 / 960 / 1600).
  • Hero LCP image preloaded.
  • Before/after images same dimensions per pair, lazy-loaded except the first pair (eager).
  • Alt text describes the floor + location, never the brand.

Form submission flow

1. User submits form
2. JS validates required fields client-side
3. POST application/x-www-form-urlencoded to Apps Script Web App URL
   (urlencoded avoids CORS preflight , keeps Apps Script simple)
4. Apps Script doPost(e):
     - validates fields, checks honeypot + timestamp
     - appends row to Google Sheet
     - GmailApp.sendEmail to byronfloorcleaning@gmail.com
     - returns { ok: true }
5. JS swaps form for in-page thank-you state
6. Fallback: form action="thanks.html" if JS fails

Apps Script Web App must be deployed as "Anyone, even anonymous" with execution as the Sheet owner. Keep the Web App URL in a config.js file the dev can swap.

Accessibility

  • Semantic landmarks (header, main, nav, footer).
  • All form fields have visible labels. No placeholder-only labels.
  • Focus states visible on every interactive element (2px gold outline).
  • Color contrast ≥ AA on every text + background pair.
  • Before/after slider is keyboard operable (arrow keys move handle).
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected.

Performance

  • Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95 on all four metrics.
  • LCP < 2.5s on 4G. CLS < 0.05.
  • Total transfer first paint ≤ 200 KB. No frameworks. No web fonts beyond Inter + Fraunces (subsetted).

Deployment

  • Push to GitHub. Connect Cloudflare Pages (or Netlify).
  • Add CNAME on GoDaddy: www → byronfloorcleaning.pages.dev; ANAME/ALIAS apex to Cloudflare.
  • Add robots.txt + sitemap.xml.
  • Submit to Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools. Verify Google Business Profile linkage.
  • Total monthly cost: $0 hosting + $20/year domain (already paid) + $0 forms.
